Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, and delivery address. We also store cart and wishlist data locally in your browser to keep your session working across visits.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your order.
  • To confirm order details and respond to queries over WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To send updates about your order status.
  • To improve our products and website, when you choose to share feedback.

How We Share Your Information

We share your delivery details with our courier partners solely to fulfil your order. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Local Storage & Cookies

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember items in your cart and wishlist. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers until you complete checkout.

Data Retention

We retain order information for as long as needed to fulfil the order, handle any exchanges or refunds, and meet our accounting obligations.

Your Rights

You may ask us to update or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us. Note that some information may need to be retained where required by law.

Contact Us

For any privacy related question, reach out through our contact page.

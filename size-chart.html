How to Measure

  • Waist: Measure straight across the waistband, just below the belt loops, then double it.
  • Hip: Measure straight across the widest part of the hip area, then double it.
  • Inseam: Measure from the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg opening.
  • Chest: Measure straight across, one inch below the armhole, then double it.

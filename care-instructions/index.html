Keep It Fresh

Care Instructions

Cotton Pants & 6-Pocket Pants

  • Machine wash cold with like colors.
  • Tumble dry on low, or line dry to preserve the fabric.
  • Do not use bleach or fabric softener on printed panels.
  • Iron on medium heat, inside out, if needed.

Denim Jeans

  • Wash inside out in cold water to protect the wash and any topstitching.
  • Wash sparingly. Spot cleaning between wears extends the life of the denim.
  • Line dry whenever possible to prevent shrinkage.

Baggy Pants

  • Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle.
  • Close any zips and turn pockets inside before washing.
  • Avoid high heat drying, which can affect the drape of the fabric.

T-Shirts

  • Machine wash cold, inside out, to protect prints and embroidery.
  • Do not iron directly on printed graphics.
  • Tumble dry low or hang dry for the longest lifespan.

General Tips

Always check the care label sewn into each garment, as it reflects the specific fabric blend used for that piece. When in doubt, a cold wash and air dry is the safest option for anything in your 9EXO wardrobe.
